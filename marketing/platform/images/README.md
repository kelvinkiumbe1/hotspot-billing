# Photographs this page wants

The three `photo-*.jpg` files are **placeholders**, copied from images already in
this folder so nothing is broken. Replace them with real photographs, keeping the
same filenames — nothing in the HTML needs to change.

| File | What it should show | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `photo-market.jpg` | A busy African street or market with small shops — the kind of place a hotspot gets sold | Full-bleed divider between Features and Payments |
| `photo-pay.jpg` | A hand entering a PIN on a phone, ideally a budget Android or feature phone | Payments section |
| `photo-tech.jpg` | A technician on a rooftop or mast fitting a dish or antenna | Full-bleed divider before How it works |

## Rules for anything added here

**1600px wide, landscape, JPEG, under ~250 KB.** The dividers crop to about
330px tall and sit under a dark gradient, so avoid anything with detail in the
centre — the caption sits there.

**No other company's branding.** `p3.jpg` is a genuinely good MikroTik rack shot
and cannot be used: it carries "nextlink NETWORKING" three times.

**Nothing with a Western skyline.** `p11.jpg` is Manhattan at night. `p9.jpg` is
purple-lit datacentre cooling that also fights the yellow accent. Both are in
this folder and both are unreferenced on purpose.

**Warm light beats cool.** The page's accent is #fdbf2d; blue-hour and magenta
stock clashes with it.
