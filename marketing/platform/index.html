Money in

Get paid the way your customers already pay

Thirteen rails, thirty-one countries. Switch on as many as you like at once — your customers pick at checkout, and whichever you list first is what USSD and WhatsApp use.

A prompt on their phone

The customer types their PIN and nothing else happens — no page to open, no card to find. M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and EcoCash all work this way.

A page they open

Wallets, cards, bank transfer and USSD on one hosted checkout. Orange Money, Wave, Paystack, Flutterwave, Chapa and Stripe — including the markets where cards and bank transfer, not mobile money, are what people actually use.

Paybill, till and bank

No API keys and no aggregator. Show customers your paybill, your Buy Goods till or your bank account and reconcile against it — a customer can even claim access with the code from their own confirmation SMS.

Three switched on at once, and the customer chooses. Nothing here is a mock-up.

Outside Africa, Zidi still bills. Card payments work anywhere Stripe does, and an operator in a country not listed above gets the generic setup — their own currency, English, and cards. What Africa gets on top is the local rails: the wallet your customers already keep their money in, rather than a card most of them do not have.

Automations that protect and grow your revenue

Zidi doesn't just take payments — it chases the money you'd otherwise lose, wins customers back, enforces your rules and keeps you compliant, all on its own.

Recovers failed payments

When an auto-renewal doesn't go through, Zidi retries on a smart schedule with a tap-to-pay prompt — so a customer who simply had no float doesn't quietly churn. New

Renewals that collect themselves

A customer agrees once and the monthly payment simply arrives — an M-Pesa standing order they approve on their handset, or a saved method Zidi charges on the day. Nobody gets chased, because there is nothing to chase. New

Wins back lapsed customers

Gone quiet? An escalating come-back series reaches out automatically over the following weeks — and stops the instant they return.

Smart top-up nudges

Customers get a heads-up just before their time or data runs out — a reply on WhatsApp tops them up without logging in again.

Fair-use, enforced

Set a data cap once. At the limit Zidi throttles, blocks or just notifies — whichever you chose — with no manual policing.

Catches code sharing

One voucher lighting up several devices at once? You're flagged the moment it happens — protect the revenue you're owed. New

Self-healing network

After a router reboot, paid customers reconnect with their remaining time and lapsed ones stay locked out — the network puts itself right.
